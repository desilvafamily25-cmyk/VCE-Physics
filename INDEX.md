## Where the solutions are

VCAA does not publish separate answer sheets. **The examination report *is* the
solutions document.** Each `*-report.*` file sits beside its paper and contains:

- **Section A (multiple choice)** — the correct option for every question, plus
  the percentage of students who chose each option, plus examiner commentary.
- **Section B (short answer)** — sample answers, the mark distribution
  (% of students scoring 0, 1, 2 …), the average mark, and notes on common errors.

So `2025-physics-exam.pdf` pairs with `2025-physics-report.docx`, and so on.

## Important caveats

- **2012 and earlier had two papers** (Exam 1 mid-year, Exam 2 end-of-year), so
  those years have `-exam1`/`-exam2` and matching `-report1`/`-report2`.
- **Content differs by design period.** The current design examines things older
  papers never tested (photovoltaic cells and inverters, for example), and older
  papers test material no longer examinable. Never practise pre-2024 questions
  without checking them against the current key knowledge.
- `reference/sample-exam-2024.pdf` has no published solutions.

## Why the report data is valuable beyond the answers

The per-option percentages and mark distributions are **empirical difficulty and
discrimination data**. A question where 51% chose the right answer and 27% chose
one particular distractor tells you both how hard it is and precisely which
misconception the cohort holds — far better evidence than an expert difficulty
guess.
